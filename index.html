Get to know us

Photo of David

I grew up in Santa Barbara and Ventura, surrounded by the beauty of the coast and mountains, and that's where my love for landscaping really started. I've always believed that outdoor spaces should feel both inviting and unique, and I enjoy helping people bring their ideas to life in a way that feels true to them.

With over 16 years of hands-on experience working alongside general contractors, masons, and other landscape professionals, I've seen projects from every angle — and that gives me a practical, well-rounded approach. I value clear communication, attention to detail, and making sure every client feels taken care of from start to finish.

At the end of the day, I want to create spaces you'll truly enjoy spending time in, whether it's a quiet retreat or a lively spot for gatherings.
